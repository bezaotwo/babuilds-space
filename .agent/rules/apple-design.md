## Apple Design & HIG Standards
When building and styling components:
- Reference `.design-rules/SKILL.md` and `.design-rules/references/hig/` for layout, hierarchy, and materials.
- Maintain a minimum interactive touch target of 44×44 pt (`min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px]`).
- Maintain text contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
- Use translucent elevated materials (`backdrop-blur`) for functional navigation and clean elevated surfaces for content cards.
